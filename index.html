A little clarity

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Is PATHOS a medical device?+

No. It’s a wellness awareness research project. It does not diagnose and is not a substitute for care.

Who is this being built for?+

Nonverbal and neurodivergent people, and the caregivers, teachers and therapists around them. Community conversations help guide the work.

What works today, and what’s next?+

MVP 1 connects an ESP32 controller and BME280 room sensor to the dashboard. Pulse, skin conductance, skin temperature, and motion sensing are in development for MVP 2, along with a wearable case and caregiver alerts.

Does this website measure my stress?+

No. The planet and traces are illustrations. This website is not connected to a sensor and collects no physiological measurements from visitors.

Where does the model run?+

The model is designed to run on the device. Network alerts for caregivers are planned for MVP 2. The research has not yet been validated on PATHOS hardware.

Can I see a demo or get involved?+

Yes. Get in touch about a demonstration, research collaboration, nonprofit partnership, sponsorship, or a conversation about the work.
